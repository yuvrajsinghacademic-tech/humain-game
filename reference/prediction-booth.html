The Prediction Booth

Two machines. Pull whichever lever you like. Before you choose, Claude writes a sealed guess about which one you're about to pick — based only on watching what you've done so far in this game.

Inspired by Binz et al.'s Centaur model (Nature, 2025), which was fine-tuned on 10M+ real human choices across 160 experiments. This demo doesn't fine-tune anything — it just prompts Claude with the trial history and asks it to guess your next move, live. Think of it as a rough stand-in for the paper's un-finetuned baseline model, not for Centaur itself.
